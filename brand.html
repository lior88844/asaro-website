Color palette

Type that goes with it

Display · headings, names, quotes
Cormorant Garamond Bold

The logo's own typeface. Use it for headings, dish names and short quotes. Next to the logo, space it wide (about 0.12em). Free on Google Fonts.

Text · body, labels, buttons
Jost

A clean sans-serif for paragraphs, descriptions, prices, and small capital labels and buttons (spaced 0.2em). Regular for small text, Light for larger paragraphs. Free on Google Fonts.

A few rules

  • One background. Oak everywhere; separate sections with space and thin cream lines at 12%, not with new colors.
  • Tan is for emphasis. One or two tan words per heading at most, so it keeps its pull.
  • Photos stay warm. Black-and-white images are toned oak to cream so they sit in the palette.
  • No pure black or pure white. Use Oak, Ink and Cream instead; they keep everything soft and rustic.
